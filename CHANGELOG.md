## 0.0.1

* Initial release of `haptic_swipe_slider`.
* Liquid and ergonomic slide-to-confirm pill slider widget.
* Magnetic drag physics with smooth spring-back animation.
* Multi-stage tactile haptic feedback (start, milestones, confirm, cancel).
* Dynamic trail fill with optional shimmer sweep lighting.
* Integrated asynchronous loading spinner and success checkmark states.
* Programmatic `HapticSwipeSliderController` for full lifecycle management.
* Comprehensive styling, color gradients, icons, and RTL/reversed slide support.
